# Journal: RSNA knee campaign

This journal holds sanitized snapshots of the campaign documents for the RSNA knee abnormality detection competition. The snapshot date is 2026-10-10. The official-row data is current to the campaign ledger at 2026-10-10 11:27:29 UTC. The coordinator for this campaign was an AI coding agent working under the owner's direction.

Private worker identities, credentials, local paths and internal session identifiers are removed. Scores are the public scores Kaggle displays. Each one covers part of the test set and is rounded to three decimals. Rows without a public score are not results, and this journal does not treat them as results.

## Files

- `2026-10-08-frontier-sweep.md`: the public frontier on the day of the read-only sweep, the leaderboard context, and what was verified or refuted.
- `2026-10-09-strategy.md`: the strategy synthesis (levers kept, refuted and repaired, execution order, probabilities as stated by the sources), with a status section at the snapshot.
- `2026-10-10-state.md`: every official row to date with its role and raw status, the efficiency-board facts as the ledger states them, what is running and what is next.
- `lessons.md`: engineering lessons from the hidden reruns, canary limits, fail-closed gates, shared state, notebook identity and session limits, each with its evidence and check.
- `README.md`: this index.

## Label legend

- [V] checked against source, metadata, a rendered page or the campaign ledger
- [P] another author's claim (a forum post, notebook card or write-up)
- [I] the author's inference or estimate

The strategy synthesis defines [V] as verified in the ledger or in a verdict note. Its tags are kept as written.
