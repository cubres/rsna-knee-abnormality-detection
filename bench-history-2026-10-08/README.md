## Bench version 18: the ConvNeXt arm proven, and the blend measured

After the two failures above, version 18 of the bench ran the ConvNeXt arm end to end: per-study probabilities equal to the reader author's own visible output to 3.0e-8, decoders present in the container, zero decode failures, 7 s per batch. Its official row **56955785 scored 0.948** ([evidence/v18-row-complete.json](evidence/v18-row-complete.json)), one tick below the mirror reader alone (0.949). Under the predeclared rule the 0.70 / 0.30 global blend is **not adopted**. The difference is inside a one-tick noise band on the public split, so this does not show the arm is harmful; it shows that a single global weight of 0.30 does not reproduce the public 0.950 cards, which use per-label hand-set or gold-58-tuned weights that we deliberately did not copy. The public notebook's version 35 carries the identical blend and is being scored; its head will be returned to the mirror-only path in the next version with this result stated.
